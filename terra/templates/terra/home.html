01 — Manifesto

We were never meant to live on only one world.

Terra Nova was found the honest way — by an array of telescopes listening for the colour of water. In 2025 the LAGOON survey returned a spectrum that no model could explain away: shallow seas, free oxygen, a magnetosphere holding the line.

So we are going. Not because this world failed, but because a species with one habitat is a species one accident from silence. The first thousand passengers will land at Aurelia Basin with seeds, printers and the patience to do it better.

“Terra Nova is not an escape. It is a second draft, written in the same hand — gentler this time.” — LAGOON CHARTER, ART. I

03 — Program

The road to first footsteps.

  1. 2023

    The LAGOON array wakes

    Ninety-six dishes across the southern hemisphere begin listening for the colour of water in 2,400 nearby systems.

  2. 2025

    Terra Nova confirmed

    Free oxygen, liquid seas, a magnetosphere holding the line. The spectrum no model could explain away gets a name.

  3. 2026

    Expedition 01 opens NOW

    The passenger registry goes live. One thousand passages, assigned sectors, callsigns issued on confirmation.

  4. 2029

    Touchdown at Aurelia Basin

    First landing party steps onto warm sand with seeds, printers and the patience to do it better this time.

  5. 2034

    The first town

    A thousand researchers, a harbour, and the first child to hold a passport issued by two worlds.
